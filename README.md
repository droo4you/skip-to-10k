# Skip to 10K

A Droober & RC rock-skipping saga. In 2017 somebody made 100 rocks. They do nothing. Droober found one by the lake.

Throw it, skip it across eleven years of crypto history, and try to break $10K. The chart goes sideways a lot.

- Aim: drag back and let go, or UP/DOWN + hold SPACE.
- Fly: SPACE (or tap) as the rock hits the water for a perfect skip. UP/DOWN tilt. X fart boost. C calls RC.
- Spend gwei at the dock shop between runs.

Run it: `node serve.mjs` then open http://localhost:5178. By droober.
